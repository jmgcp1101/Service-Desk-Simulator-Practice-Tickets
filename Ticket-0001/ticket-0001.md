What I Did

The PC was completely unresponsive, with no lights, fans, or other signs of power. I reviewed the documentation provided with the ticket and worked through the available information to determine the most practical solution. Since the machine showed no signs of powering on and the employee needed a working system as soon as possible, I ultimately built a replacement PC and arranged for it to be shipped to the employee.

What I Got Stuck On / What I Learned

My main challenge was interpreting the clues provided in the employee’s report. I initially kept second-guessing whether replacing the PC was really the right approach, which made me spend more time analyzing the situation than necessary. This ticket helped me realize the importance of looking at the symptoms and available information as a whole, rather than overthinking one possible solution.
