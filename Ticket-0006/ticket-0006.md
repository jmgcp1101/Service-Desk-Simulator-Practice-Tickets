Ticket Summary

Maria Garcia, a lead developer, was unable to log in because her password had expired after an extended absence. The issue prevented her from accessing her development environment ahead of an upcoming sprint deadline. The password could not be changed from the login screen, so I verified her identity through MFA, reset the password using the Directory, and securely provided her with a temporary password.

The main challenge was initially identifying the correct tool and troubleshooting approach. After consulting the knowledge base, I determined that the issue could be resolved through the Directory with the user's cooperation, without requiring remote access.

What I did:
I contacted the user associated with the ticket through Company Chat and verified their identity using the available MFA verification process. After successful verification, I used the Directory to reset the user's expired password and generated a temporary password. I then provided the temporary password directly to the user through a secure communication channel and instructed them to change it upon their next login.

What I initially got wrong / where I got stuck:
