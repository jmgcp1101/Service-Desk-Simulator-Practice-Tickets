Ticket Summary

The employee was transferred from the Engineering Department to IT Infrastructure and needed her access updated to match her new role. Her previous Engineering access also needed to be revoked in accordance with the company's security policy.

What I Did

I started by checking the employee's information in the directory. I found that she was still assigned to the Engineering Department, which explained why her access had not been updated.

I removed her previous Engineering role and assigned her to IT Infrastructure. This automatically updated her access permissions to match her new role, while removing access associated with her previous department.

The employee's access was successfully updated and the issue was resolved.

What I Did Wrong / Where I Got Stuck

For this ticket, I didn't encounter much difficulty because checking the directory has already become one of my first troubleshooting steps. Since the request specifically involved a department transfer and access modification, checking the employee's directory information was a logical starting point.

One thing I can improve is being more deliberate about why I'm checking a particular system rather than relying purely on instinct. In this case, however, that instinct led directly to the correct area and helped resolve the ticket quickly.

Key Takeaway

Department transfers can affect both access provisioning and access revocation. When handling these requests, verify the user's current department/role and make sure their permissions align with their new responsibilities while removing unnecessary access from their previous role.
